FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about partnering with Neovis for enterprise AI development, security compliance, and deployment.

How does Neovis ensure data security and privacy for enterprise data?

Security is foundational to our engineering methodology. We deploy completely isolated, tenant-private cloud environments or air-gapped models. Your proprietary enterprise data is never transmitted to public model training sets or stored on unverified third-party servers. All data transfers utilize TLS 1.3 encryption, and operations adhere strictly to SOC2 and GDPR compliance standards.

What is the typical onboarding and implementation timeline?

Our 4-stage engineering process delivers an initial functional prototype within 2 weeks. Full system integration, model fine-tuning, and production launch typically take 4 to 6 weeks, depending on the scope of your custom database connections and API webhook workflows.

What pricing and licensing models do you offer?

We offer transparent, milestone-based fixed pricing for initial solution development, paired with flexible monthly maintenance and SLA packages for ongoing model optimization, telemetry monitoring, and serverless hosting management. You retain 100% intellectual property rights to custom code built for your organization.

Can Neovis integrate with our existing software and databases?

Yes. Our engines are built with clean RESTful, GraphQL, and WebSocket API connectors. Whether you operate on legacy relational databases, cloud data warehouses, custom CRMs, or modern React/Node web platforms, Neovis AI engines integrate directly into your existing software stack without requiring expensive infrastructure overhauls.

What ongoing maintenance and support is provided after launch?

Every production deployment includes dedicated post-launch support, 24/7 automated uptime and latency monitoring, continuous prompt/weight optimization, and quarterly security audits to ensure your autonomous AI engines remain high-performing as your business scales.
